About Meeple

Welcome to Meeple’s 🎲

Your Weekly Tabletop Lounge & Social Club in Komatsugawa–Higashi-Ojima

Meeple’s was built on a simple idea: board games are better when played together. Whether you're looking to unwind after a busy week of work or classes, meet friendly locals, or dive into strategy and card games, Meeple’s is your weekend spot to unplug and connect.

🌟 About Meeple’s

What We’re All About

Meeple’s is a free, casual board game club created specifically for teens and young adults (ages 12+). We meet weekly for 1.5-hour drop-in sessions across local community spaces in the Komatsugawa and Higashi-Ojima areas—including neighborhood halls (JKK Hall, Komatsugawa Sakura Hall) and outdoor sessions at Ōjima-Komatsugawa Park on sunny days!

What We Play

Our collection includes a mix of strategy, dexterity, and casual party games:

Classics & Strategy: Catan, Carcassonne, Ticket to Ride, Monopoly, Scotland Yard, Othello, Chess

Skill & Dexterity: Carrom, Gravity Maze

Casual & Cards: Playing cards, UNO, and quick-play party games

Bring Your Own Game (BYOG): Have a favorite game at home collecting dust? Bring it along!

📜 House Rules

To keep Meeple’s safe, clean, and welcoming for everyone, we ask all members to follow a few simple guidelines:

1. No Alcohol 🚫 — To maintain an inclusive and safe environment for ages 12+, alcohol is strictly prohibited at all hall and park sessions.

2. BYO Snacks & Drinks 🥤 — Feel free to bring your own soft drinks or snacks! Please use covered cups/bottles and keep greasy food away from game boards and cards.

3. Respect Games & Players 🧩 — Handle game pieces with care and practice good sportsmanship. Everyone is here to de-stress and have fun!

4. Drop-In & Leave Anytime 🚪 — No rigid attendance rules. Join when you can and head out when you need to—just let your game table know before starting a long round.

5. Leave No Trace 🧹 — We respect our local public halls and parks. Please clean up your table and pack out your own trash (gomi) after each session.
